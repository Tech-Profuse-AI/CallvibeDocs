# Email

The **Email** page lists the customer email conversations from your team's shared inboxes, with an AI summary of each one. Use it to see which customers are waiting for a reply and what each conversation needs. Open it from Email in the sidebar. The page opens with the line "What customers are emailing about, and which conversations are waiting on you."

## What You See

At the top are summary figures:

- **Conversations:** How many conversations match the period you chose.
- **Waiting on us:** How many are waiting for your reply.
- **Analysed:** How many the AI has analysed, for example "20 of 20 conversations".
- **Sentiment:** A breakdown of positive, neutral, and negative conversations across the analysed ones.

Below is the conversation list. Each row is one conversation, with these columns:

- **Conversation:** The subject, with the sender's name and email underneath. When the sender is linked to a contact, the contact's name shows below. Tick the box at the start of a row to select it.
- **Needs:** What it is about, shown as a tag such as Complaint or International Patient. **Needs reply** shows how long the customer has been waiting, for example 2d 01h. An **Alert** tag means someone should act now.
- **Mailbox:** The inbox it arrived in.
- **Source:** Where the conversation came from.
- **Emails:** How many emails it holds.
- **Sentiment:** How the customer feels.
- **Summary:** A short AI summary of where the conversation stands, and the next step.
- **Last email:** When the last email arrived.

## Find a Conversation

Use the filters at the top:

- **Period:** Today, Last 3 days, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, or All time.
- **Status:** All conversations, **Waiting on us**, **Needs a contact**, **Internal**, or **Not analysed**.
- **Sentiment:** Positive, Neutral, or Negative.
- **Mailbox:** One inbox.
- **Agent:** One team member.
- **Search:** By subject, name, or email.

## Open a Conversation

Click a conversation to read it. The conversation view shows:

- **The analysis:** The sentiment, when it was analysed, the summary, and the next action. It says "No action needed" when nothing is waiting.
- **The email thread:** Every email in the conversation, with its pictures and attachments.

## Link a Conversation to a Contact

A conversation marked **Needs a contact** has not been matched to anyone. Tick it, then use the button that appears to assign it to a contact, and pick the right one. Once linked, the conversation appears on that contact's timeline.

## Email Settings

Owners can open **Email settings** in Settings > Integrations to:

- List your team's agents and team domains, so your own replies and team-only conversations are not treated as customers.
- List senders to skip, such as automatic mail. Their mail is kept but not analysed, and you can show it with the **Not analysed** filter.

## What You Get From It

- A list of customers waiting on a reply, so no email is missed.
- The mood of your email conversations at a glance.
- A summary and next step for each conversation, so you don't have to read every email.
- Emails shown on the contact's timeline, with their calls, chats, and meetings.

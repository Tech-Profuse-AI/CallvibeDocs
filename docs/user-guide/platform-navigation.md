# Platform Navigation

The left sidebar is split into Main, Channels, and Workspace.

## Main

- Overview: the landing page. Call rates against targets, volume, AI analysis coverage, and whether data is arriving. The **Across every channel** section shows how WhatsApp, email, and meetings went next to your calls. A channel that isn't set up is left out
- Outlook: an AI summary of a day, week, or month, with patterns and what customers wanted. See [Outlook](./outlook.md)
- Intelligence: Call quality scores over time, how scores are distributed, and attainment by agent
- Contacts: Contact-centric history and context. See [Contacts](#contacts)
- Follow-ups: Follow-up actions raised from calls. The sidebar badge shows the open count
- Performance: Agent-level volume and quality. Agents see only their own numbers

## Channels

- Calls: Search, filter, and inspect call records
- WhatsApp: Chat threads and message analytics. The sidebar badge shows unread messages
- Email: Customer email conversations, with a summary, sentiment, and whether the customer is waiting for a reply
- Meetings: Your online meetings with a transcript, key points, and action items

See [Channels](/docs/category/channels) for how to connect each one.

## Workspace

Visible to owner role.

- Team & access: User invites, role management, and agent-name mappings
- Settings: Preferences, Integrations, Meeting recorder, AI Insights, Notifications, Customer Management, Lead Statuses, Lead Custom Fields, Marketing Sources, and Usage

## Contacts

Contacts has five tabs:

- **Contacts:** The list of contacts. Filter by stage, owner, time of last interaction, and channel (Calls, WhatsApp, Email, Meetings). You can hide internal lines.
- **Scheduled:** Calls scheduled with contacts.
- **Pipeline:** Contacts laid out by lead stage.
- **Reports:** Contact reports.
- **Cleanup:** Tidy up contacts, for example adding an email address to a contact.

Open a contact to see their calls, chats, emails, and meetings together.

## Top Bar

- Data health pill: shows whether calls are arriving. Select it to open Integrations. See [Data Health](../integrations/overview.mdx#data-health)
- Ask: opens the in-app assistant in a side panel

:::note

Older links still work and take you to the new page. For example, Call Records opens Calls, Customers and Leads open Contacts, and Action Items opens Follow-ups.

:::

# WhatsApp

The **WhatsApp** page lists the chats between your team and your customers, with an AI summary of each one. Use it to see which customers are waiting for a reply and how conversations are going. Open it from WhatsApp in the sidebar. The page opens with the line "What customers are messaging about, and how those conversations are going." The sidebar badge shows unread messages.

## What You See

At the top are summary figures:

- **Chats:** How many chats match the period you chose.
- **Analysed:** How many of them the AI has analysed, for example "40 of 40 chats".
- **Total messages:** The number of messages in those chats.
- **Sentiment:** A breakdown of positive, neutral, and negative chats across the analysed ones.

Below is the chat list. Each row is one chat, with these columns:

- **Customer:** Who the chat is with.
- **Needs:** What the chat is about, shown as a tag such as Appointment Booking or Insurance & Billing. An **Alert** tag means someone should act now.
- **Account:** The WhatsApp account the chat is on.
- **Messages:** How many messages it holds.
- **Sentiment:** How the customer feels.
- **Summary:** A short AI summary of where the chat stands, and the next step.
- **Last activity:** When the last message arrived.

A chat that has not been analysed yet shows "Not analysed".

## Find a Chat

Use the filters at the top:

- **Period:** Today, Last 3 days, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, or All time.
- **Status:** All chats, or **Waiting on us** for chats where the customer is waiting for a reply.
- **Sentiment:** Positive, Neutral, or Negative.
- **Account:** One WhatsApp account.
- **Search:** By name or phone.

## Open a Chat

Click a chat to read it. The chat view shows:

- **The analysis:** The sentiment, when it was analysed, the summary, and the next action.
- **The messages:** The whole conversation, with photos, audio, video, and documents.

## Analyse Chats

CallVibe analyses chats on its own. Two buttons at the top let you do it yourself:

- **Analyse chats:** Analyses chats that have not been analysed yet.
- **Force analyse:** Analyses the most recent chats again with the latest AI. This covers the last 30 days, up to 100 chats, and can take up to a minute.

## What You Get From It

- A clear list of customers waiting on you, so no message is missed.
- The mood of your WhatsApp conversations at a glance.
- A summary and next step for each chat, so you don't have to read every message.
- Chats shown on the contact's timeline, with their calls, emails, and meetings.

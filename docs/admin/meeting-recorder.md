# Meeting Recorder

The CallVibe Recorder is a Chrome extension that records your meeting audio without adding a bot to the meeting. Open its rules from Settings > Meeting recorder. These rules apply to your whole team and override each person's own extension settings. Only owners can open this page.

## Connect the Extension

1. Install the CallVibe Recorder in Chrome.
2. Open the extension and select **Connect to CallVibe**.
3. On the CallVibe page that opens, check the code matches the one in the extension, then approve.

Nothing records until the extension is connected.

## Recording

- **Record Google Meet and Teams automatically:** Recording starts when someone joins, with no click. Turn it off and people click the CallVibe icon to record. Zoom always needs the click. Only turn this on if your team tells participants they are being recorded.
- **Find who was invited in the recorder's calendar:** After a meeting, the invited people are used to link the meeting to your contacts and to mark a team-only meeting as internal.
- **Platforms that may be recorded:** Choose Google Meet, Zoom (web), and Microsoft Teams (web).

## Telling Participants

- **Consent notice (optional):** Saved with every recording.
- **Post a notice in the Google Meet chat:** When recording starts, the extension posts your **Chat message** in the meeting chat. Google Meet only.

## Connected Browsers

Each browser that is connected to your account is listed here. Select **Disconnect** to stop uploads from a browser straight away.

## Team List

The Meeting team list holds the people on your team. A meeting with only these people is kept but not analysed.
